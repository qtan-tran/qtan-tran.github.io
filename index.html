About me

I am a post-doctoral researcher at the Institute for Studies of Science (ISoS), Bielefeld University. My work sits at the intersection of science and technology studies, infrastructure studies, and the sociology of scientific knowledge, with a focus on data infrastructures and open culture. Since November 2024, I have been studying how Open Access policies interact with research evaluation and publishing practices in the ROARA project. Before that, I researched participatory memory practices in European museums as a Marie Curie fellow in the POEM network at the University of Hamburg (2018–2022), drawing on ethnographic fieldwork in Germany, Sweden, and Scotland.

Research projects

ROARA: Repercussions of Open Access on Research Assessment Current

Post-doctoral research project · Bielefeld University · since November 2024

How do Open Access policies reshape research evaluation? I conduct qualitative interviews with publishers, journal editors, and researchers, and analyze science policy documents from Germany, the Netherlands, Colombia, South Africa, and Indonesia. The project examines the links between Open Access policy, emerging questionable publication practices, and research evaluation.

POEM: Participatory Memory Practices

Ph.D. research project · Horizon 2020 Innovative Training Network · University of Hamburg · 2018–2022

I studied museum infrastructures in Europe using multi-sited ethnography, semi-structured interviews, and grounded theory. Based on 30 interviews with cultural heritage professionals in Germany, Sweden, and Scotland, the project assessed public participation in information access and heritage representation across the EU.

Repositories

Tools and datasets from my research and teaching.

  • oa-explorer

    A durable, free-to-use explorer for the ROARA corpus of open-access and open-science policies: filter, search, and open versioned policy records with evidence, original-language excerpts, and source anchors; follow relationships across policy versions; and download cautious, transparent exports. In development as part of the ROARA project; the research corpus lives in the companion repository oa-os-policy-collections.

    GitHub

  • graecum-vn

    A free portal for self-study of Ancient Greek in Vietnamese: 48 lessons across two levels (Graecum I and II), a parallel English edition, and the ancient-greek-toolkit hub of study tools.
